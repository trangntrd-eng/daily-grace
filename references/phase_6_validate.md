# Phase 6: Validate & Align

**Goal**: To align all stakeholders on the proposed solution and ensure the technical team is ready for execution.

**Owners**: The **Product Specialist (PS)** leads the stakeholder alignment process, while the **Tech Lead (TL)** leads the technical preparation.

## Key Steps

### 6.1: Stakeholder Alignment (🔵 PRODUCT-TECH CHECKPOINT)
This is a critical checkpoint to ensure that all relevant stakeholders are aware of and aligned on the feature that is about to be built.

- The Product Specialist should present the Design Document from Phase 5 to all stakeholders, including Product, Tech, Design, Marketing, and any other affected teams.
- The goal is to gather feedback, address any concerns, and get buy-in from all parties.

### 6.2: Kick-off Readiness Check
Before the official kick-off, the Product Specialist and Tech Lead should conduct a final readiness check.

- **Product Readiness**: Is the PRD ready? Are the user stories written? Is the success measurement plan in place?
- **Tech Readiness**: Is the team staffed? Are the technical dependencies understood? Is the development environment ready?

### 6.3: Execution Plan Review
The Tech Lead should present a high-level execution plan, including:
- A timeline with key milestones.
- A breakdown of the work into epics and stories.
- A plan for testing and quality assurance.

## Phase 6 Checkpoint (Product-Tech Aligned)

Before proceeding, ensure the following conditions are met:

- [ ] All stakeholders are aligned on the recommended solution.
- [ ] The kick-off readiness check has been completed.
- [ ] The execution plan has been reviewed and agreed upon.

**Output**: A **Kick-off Ready Document** that confirms all parties are aligned and ready to begin development.
