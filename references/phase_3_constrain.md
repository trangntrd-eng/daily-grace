# Phase 3: Constrain

**Goal**: For the Tech team to provide realistic technical constraints, enabling the Product team to design within the boundaries of what is feasible.

**Owner**: The **Tech Lead (TL)** is the primary owner of this phase, responsible for providing the constraints. The **Product Specialist (PS)** participates to understand the design space.

## Key Steps

### 3.1: Constraint Workshop (🔵 PRODUCT-TECH CHECKPOINT)
This is a mandatory workshop or asynchronous document review where the Tech Lead provides answers to key constraint questions based on the validated Techstack Track.

- **Input**: The Track Mapping Document from Phase 2.
- **Format**: Can be a live workshop or an async document exchange, depending on the team's preference.
- **Tech Lead's Role**: To answer the constraint questions relevant to their specific track. This is a tech-led step, not a product-led one.

For each track, specific questions need to be answered. These are detailed in the `techstack_tracks.md` reference file.

### 3.2: Spectrum of Choices
The Tech Lead should outline a spectrum of possible implementation options, from the simplest to the most complex.

- **Option 1 (Lean)**: The simplest, quickest implementation. What are its limitations?
- **Option 2 (Mid)**: A balanced approach. What are the trade-offs?
- **Option 3 (Full)**: The most comprehensive and complex implementation. What are the risks?

This provides the Product team with a clear understanding of the trade-offs between effort, risk, and functionality.

## Phase 3 Checkpoint (Tech-led)

Before moving on, ensure the following conditions are met:

- [ ] The Product team understands the practical design space.
- [ ] The Tech team has clearly communicated the trade-offs and dependencies.
- [ ] A spectrum of implementation choices has been presented.

**Output**: A **Tech Constraint Document**, written by the Tech Lead, which clearly lists all technical limitations, risks, dependencies, and feasible implementation paths.
