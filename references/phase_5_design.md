# Phase 5: Design & Diverge

**Goal**: For the Product team to design multiple potential solutions based on the established constraints, and for the Tech team to provide feasibility feedback on each.

**Owners**: The **Product Specialist (PS)** leads the design process. The **Tech Lead (TL)** is responsible for validating the feasibility of the proposed designs.

## Key Steps

### 5.1: Design Multiple Solutions (Diverge)
Instead of settling on the first idea, the Product Specialist should design 2-3 different solutions that address the root problem. These solutions should explore different approaches and trade-offs, leveraging the "Spectrum of Choices" provided by the Tech Lead in Phase 3.

For each solution, provide:
- A clear description of the user experience.
- Wireframes or mockups to visualize the solution.
- A summary of the pros and cons.

### 5.2: Tech Feasibility Check (🔵 PRODUCT-TECH CHECKPOINT)
This is a mandatory checkpoint for the Tech Lead to review the proposed solutions and provide feedback on their technical feasibility.

For each proposed solution, the Tech Lead should assess:
- The estimated effort (e.g., using t-shirt sizes: XS, S, M, L, XL).
- Any potential technical risks or challenges.
- Any dependencies on other teams or systems.

### 5.3: Converge to a Recommended Solution
Based on the tech feasibility feedback and a clear-eyed assessment of the trade-offs, the Product Specialist, in consultation with the Tech Lead, will select a recommended solution.

The decision should be documented, explaining why the chosen solution is the best path forward and why other solutions were not selected.

## Phase 5 Checkpoint (Product-Tech Aligned)

Before proceeding, ensure the following conditions are met:

- [ ] Multiple design solutions have been explored.
- [ ] The Tech Lead has provided feasibility feedback on each solution.
- [ ] A recommended solution has been selected and the decision has been documented.

**Output**: A **Design Document** that includes the recommended solution, wireframes/mockups, and the rationale for the decision.
